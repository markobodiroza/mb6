# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Decision makers who want AI working in their teams, not just talked about:

- Marketing leaders and their teams adopting AI in daily work
- Agency owners and operations leads rolling AI out across client work
- Founders, CEOs and operations leaders at B2B companies
- Any team that uses or wants to use AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude, in any industry

They arrive from LinkedIn, referrals, search or AI answers. They are busy, skeptical of AI hype and comparing a few AI specialists. In the first 30 seconds they want to know what Marko does, whether he understands their kind of work and what it costs.

## Product Purpose

markobodiroza.com is the personal site of Marko Bodiroza, an AI strategist and system builder with 15+ years in digital marketing. It sells strategy-led AI work: AI strategy and roadmaps, process analysis and redesign, AI systems and automation, team enablement and training, AI search visibility (AEO) and fractional AI leadership. Tool selection is part of the bigger picture, never the headline.

Success is a qualified inquiry. A visitor should be able to take the next step in whichever way suits them: fill out a contact form, book a call or send an email. (A project inquiry form on /contact sends through Formspree; a booking link is planned. The email address is never shown on the site.)

The site is tool-agnostic. It must never read as tied to one AI vendor. It is also personal, not an employer site: his current employer appears only as a work history entry.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, warm, memorable.

- **Sharp and technical:** precise, engineered, clearly someone who knows how AI actually works.
- **Warm and human:** a person you would want to work with, not an agency or a faceless brand.
- **Bold and memorable:** distinctive enough to stand out from other AI specialists.

Never call Marko a consultant anywhere on the site. Use AI Strategist and System Builder.

Voice follows the copy rules in the expertise brief: short, clear sentences; plain, literal headings in Title Case; no em dashes; no serial comma before "and"; no marketing-speak (leverage, robust, seamless, holistic, comprehensive, cutting-edge, next-gen, framework and similar). Never invent numbers, clients, results or quotes.

References: **Stripe** for confident, clear explanation with rich but disciplined detail; **Anthropic** for warm, human, editorial calm around serious technology.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI startup:** purple gradients, glowing orbs, neon on black, robot or brain imagery.
- **Template portfolio:** the stock Tailwind template look this site started from, interchangeable with thousands of developer blogs.
- **Corporate agency:** stock photos of handshakes, buzzword heroes, logo walls.
- **Guru or course seller:** countdowns, hype claims, big-number testimonials, aggressive upsells.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The site itself should show clear thinking and careful craft, the same qualities he brings to AI projects.
2. **Clarity before cleverness.** What he does, for whom and what it costs should be obvious within seconds. Personality comes through precision, not decoration.
3. **Human over hype.** Show a real person and real ways of working. No AI clichés, no inflated claims.
4. **Every page leads to a conversation.** Each page ends with an easy next step: form, call or email.
5. **Tool-agnostic and honest.** Present AI tools as options, label what is real and promise only what is in the brief.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, visible focus states and full keyboard access.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; motion is never required to understand content.
- Do not rely on color alone to convey meaning.
- Support both light and dark color schemes.
- Readable on phones first: many visitors arrive from LinkedIn on mobile.
